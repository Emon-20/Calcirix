How to Play Calcirix

1. Grid Uniqueness (Sudoku Rules)

In every puzzle, numbers 1 through N must appear exactly once in each row and column.

In Level 1 (4×4), every 2×2 block must also hold 1–4. In Level 4 (9×9), every 3×3 box must hold 1–9 exactly once!

2. Region Cages (KenKen Arithmetic)

The grid is partitioned into outlined cages with arithmetic clues (e.g. 12+, 6×, 2÷, 3-, 5):

  • + (Addition): All cell values in the cage must sum to the target.
  • × (Multiplication): All cell values multiplied together must equal the target.
  • - (Subtraction): Difference between the two cell values equals the target.
  • ÷ (Division): Larger value divided by smaller value equals the target.
  • Single numbers: Free clue! That cell must be that exact number.

3. Progressive Level System

  • Level 1 (4×4): 2×2 Sudoku blocks + KenKen arithmetic. Perfect warmup.
  • Level 2 (5×5): Intermediate Latin square + hybrid arithmetic cages.
  • Level 3 (7×7): Advanced multi-cell cages and deep logical deductions.
  • Level 4 (9×9): Expert master matrix combining full 3×3 Sudoku boxes and KenKen region targets.

4. Pro Controls & Shortcuts

  • Keyboard: Keys 1–9 to place, Backspace / 0 to clear, arrow keys to navigate.
  • Notes: Toggle N to jot candidate numbers.
  • Undo / Redo: Ctrl+Z to undo, Ctrl+Y to redo.
  • Hint: Press H for a smart guided clue.
